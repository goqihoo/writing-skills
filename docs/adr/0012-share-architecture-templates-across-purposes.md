# Share architecture templates across purposes

Status: accepted for the user-requested consolidation, 2026-10-01.

## Decision

Maintain one architecture template family under `methods/architecture-templates/assets/`, cataloged by its shared `templates.yaml`. Adapt it with the Knowledge Writer's knowledge method or the concrete-design profile. Keep company, System and Project type identities, authorities and completion conditions in their existing Writer registries; allow multiple types to reference one registered shared asset. Preserve local asset reachability checks and restrict shared references to the shared catalog.

Reuse the overall, subsystem, component and deployment templates across Knowledge and concrete design. Include stable company/System design to avoid retaining a third structural family. Retain Product-System Map, Architecture Decision and delivery entry/migration/validation assets for their distinct reader tasks. Reuse shared topic templates for scoped supporting artifacts without creating mandatory documents or new registered types.

## Reason and consequences

Separate template families duplicated architectural questions and let implementation depth drift. The user approved the Knowledge logic-chain corrections and requested merging delivery architecture. This supersedes ADR-0011's separate structural ownership and the dedicated-file aspect of ADR-0009; authority distinctions, adaptive template assignment and independently valuable Public Skills remain unchanged.

Preserve concrete baseline, technology realization, contract and transaction semantics, compatibility, migration limits, decision status and acceptance evidence through shared design prompts and purpose adaptation. Preserve Knowledge applicability, mechanisms, variants and transfer. Generate and validate actual shared links in both source and packaged layouts. Existing user documents are not moved by this resource migration.

Complete the consolidation when every affected type resolves to its shared source, old structural copies and active stale links are absent, remaining local assets are assigned, and the packaged Writers can reach templates and purpose rules. Automated checks verify routing and packaging; real generated documents remain the basis for judging writing quality.
