# Internal Document Types

This catalog is generated from the Product and Technical Writer registries. Edit the owning `references/internal-document-types.yaml` file, then regenerate this document.

A missing template means **Freeform Structure**. A configured template reports its explicit `fixed`, `sequence`, or `adaptive` policy.

## Product Documentation

Owner: `write-product-doc`

| Type | Description | Reader question | Authority | Mode | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Portfolio** (`product-portfolio`) | Govern the company's accepted Products and investment relationships. | Which Products and product lines does the company govern together, and how are they related as investments? | Company-level Product Portfolio, classification views, ownership, lifecycle, relationships, and shared constraints. | Templated / adaptive | Accepted Products, classification views, investment relationships, lifecycle, ownership, and authority are current. |
| **Products Registry** (`products-registry`) | Register accepted Products, owners, lifecycle, and navigation. | Which Products are formally accepted and where is each Product's authority? | Company registry of accepted Products and their current identity, owner, lifecycle, and navigation. | Templated / adaptive | Every listed Product is accepted, directly navigable, owned, and lifecycle-qualified. |
| **Product Governance** (`product-governance`) | Define Product decision rights, policies, evidence, and exceptions. | Who may make Product decisions, under which rules, and with what evidence and exception path? | Company Product governance without duplicating Project delivery or Technical governance. | Templated / adaptive | Decision rights, policies, evidence, exceptions, and review triggers are enforceable. |
| **Product Overview/Definition** (`product-definition`) | Define one Product's boundary, users, value, behavior, exclusions, and relationships. | What is this Product, whom does it serve, and what current promise and boundary does the company govern? | One accepted Product's current company definition, excluding a proposed change's PRD and customer-specific delivery. | Templated / adaptive | Boundary, users, value, current behavior, exclusions, lifecycle, and relationships agree. |
| **Capability Detail** (`capability-detail`) | Explain one Product capability's responsibility, behavior, rules, evidence, and evolution. | What durable Product responsibility does this capability own and how is it observed and governed? | One accepted Product capability's detail beneath the Public Capability Map authority. | Templated / adaptive | Responsibility, observable behavior, rules, relationships, evidence, and evolution are clear. |
| **Evidence Record** (`evidence-record`) | Separate Product observations, method, limits, interpretation, and decision implications. | What was observed, how reliable is it, and what Product decision may it inform? | Product evidence and its bounded interpretation, not a decision or general Knowledge claim. | Templated / adaptive | Observations, method, limits, interpretation, and decision implications remain distinct. |
| **Product Decision** (`product-decision`) | Preserve an accepted Product choice, alternatives, rationale, costs, and review triggers. | What Product choice was accepted, by whom, why, and at what cost? | One accepted Product decision and its supersession boundary. | Templated / adaptive | Choice, alternatives, rationale, costs, authority, validation, and review triggers are traceable. |
| **Product Release Plan** (`product-release-plan`) | Define release availability, evidence gates, dependencies, and stop conditions. | Under which Product conditions may this release become available? | Product release decision criteria and availability plan, excluding implementation tasks and Project delivery plans. | Templated / sequence | Availability, evidence gates, Product criteria, dependencies, decisions, and stop conditions support a release decision. |
| **Release Notes** (`release-notes`) | Record released current state and link changed Product authorities. | What Product behavior or availability actually changed in this release? | Released Product current state, distinct from the release plan and implementation log. | Templated / adaptive | Released state, scope, validity, user impact, evidence, and updated authority links are current. |
| **Product Review** (`product-review`) | Compare Product expectations with evidence and record conclusions, decisions, and follow-up. | What did the Product evidence show and which decisions follow from it? | One Product review event, preserving decision-time evidence and interpretation. | Templated / sequence | Expectations, evidence, outcomes, interpretations, decisions, and follow-up are traceable. |

## Technical Documentation

Owner: `write-technical-doc`

| Type | Description | Reader question | Authority | Mode | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical Strategy** (`technical-strategy`) | Define durable Technical direction, choices, constraints, outcomes, and review triggers. | Which durable Technical choices guide the company and what outcomes and constraints justify them? | Company Technical direction and investment choices, excluding Product strategy and implementation plans. | Freeform Structure | Direction, choices, constraints, outcomes, investment implications, authority, and review triggers agree. |
| **Technical Roadmap** (`technical-roadmap`) | Sequence Technical outcomes, dependencies, decisions, and review triggers. | Which Technical outcomes and decisions must occur in what dependency order? | Company Technical outcome sequence, excluding task plans and Product Roadmaps. | Templated / sequence | Outcomes, sequencing, dependencies, decisions, validation gates, and review triggers are explicit. |
| **System Landscape** (`system-landscape`) | Register Systems, owners, lifecycle, critical relationships, and authoritative links. | Which Systems are formally registered and how do their ownership, lifecycle, and critical relationships fit together? | Company registry of Systems and cross-System relationships, excluding internal System Architecture. | Templated / adaptive | Registered Systems, owners, lifecycle, governance state, critical relationships, and authority links are discoverable. |
| **System Profile** (`system-profile`) | Provide one Registered System's identity, responsibility, lifecycle, relationships, and navigation through its README. | What is this System, who owns it, and where are its authoritative documents? | One Registered System's concise company identity and navigation, not its detailed architecture. | Templated / adaptive | Purpose, responsibility, owner, lifecycle, relationships, governance, System Architecture, and downstream authority links are current. |
| **System Architecture** (`system-architecture`) | Describe one Registered System's stable internal architecture and evolution. | How does this System divide responsibility, own state and contracts, control behavior, fail, recover, and evolve? | One Registered System's architecture, excluding Machine Authority and Project Technical Design. | Templated / adaptive | Architecture Object and Scope, relevant domains and viewpoints, purpose, boundaries, Applications, Subsystems, information and state authority, contracts, controls, deployment and runtime behavior, decisions, validation, current state, target direction, evolution, and downstream links are decision-ready; readers can reconstruct the structure, trace representative behavior and failure, and explain the important choices. |
| **Subsystem Architecture** (`subsystem-architecture`) | Describe one justified Subsystem's stable responsibility and internal architecture. | How does this independently documented Subsystem divide responsibility and behave within its parent System? | One justified Subsystem within a Registered System, excluding component implementation and the parent System's overall architecture. | Templated / adaptive | Architecture Object and Scope, parent boundary, relevant domains and viewpoints, responsibilities, information and state authority, contracts, controls, deployment and runtime behavior, decisions, validation, evolution, and authority links are coherent at Subsystem Authority Level; readers can explain the internal mechanism and its fit within the parent System. |
| **Database Design** (`database-design`) | Design the data model, tables, constraints, access paths and transactions for a stable technical scope. | How does this database realize its owning System, module or component's data and operation requirements? | Supporting database design governed by its System or Subsystem; preserve module/component data ownership and link Machine Authority rather than creating another Authority Level. | Templated / adaptive | Data ownership, relationships, complete per-table physical field definitions, explicit integrity constraints and indexes, atomic changes, relevant conflict and change handling, and validation evidence are coherent with governing designs and available schema; unresolved definitions block schema-generation readiness and conditional topics follow actual scope. |
| **Technical Governance** (`technical-governance`) | Define company Technical decision rights, policies, evidence, exceptions, and review. | Who may make Technical decisions, under which policies, and with what evidence and exception path? | Company Technical governance across the Technical Landscape. | Freeform Structure | Decision rights, policies, evidence, exceptions, assurance, and review triggers are enforceable. |
| **Technical Standard** (`technical-standard`) | State normative rules, applicability, rationale, verification, exceptions, and evolution. | Which Technical rules apply, how are they verified, and how may exceptions be governed? | One company Technical standard without copying executable implementation truth. | Templated / adaptive | Scope, normative rules, rationale, verification, exceptions, ownership, and evolution are clear. |
| **Architecture Decision** (`architecture-decision`) | Preserve a choice, Architecture Object, governing scope, alternatives, rationale, accepted cost, authority, validation, and supersession. | Which architecture choice was accepted for which object and governing scope, why, and at what cost? | One accepted Architecture Decision at the narrowest Authority Level that governs its consequences. | Templated / adaptive | Architecture Object, governing scope, Authority Level, affected domains and viewpoints, choice, alternatives, rationale, costs, decision authority, validation, review, and supersession are traceable. |

| **Incident Record/Review** (`incident-record-review`) | Separate Technical impact, timeline, evidence, contributing conditions, response, learning, and actions. | What happened, what evidence explains it, and which governed changes follow? | One Technical incident record or review without turning hypotheses into facts or actions into proof. | Templated / sequence | Impact, timeline, evidence, contributing conditions, response, learning, decisions, and actions are distinguished. |
