# Write Product Doc

Use one Product Common Writer for registered Internal Document Types and compatible Freeform Artifacts.

## Workflow

1. Read `../../methods/product-reasoning.md` and `../../methods/internal-document-types.md`. Apply both inside this workflow.
2. Read company and Product navigation, the Product Assessment when persisted, the target file, authoritative evidence, and representative siblings.
3. Establish the named company and accepted portfolio or product boundary from the available evidence. If the subject routes elsewhere or remains materially disputed, stop because drafting would create unsupported Product Documentation; name the evidence or decision required.
4. Route Product Strategy to `$Write Product Strategy`, Capability Map to `$Map Product Capabilities`, Product Roadmap to `$Write Product Roadmap`, PRD to `$Write PRD`, and Product Metric System to `$Design Product Metrics`; return the complete invocation and stop before drafting.
5. Read `references/internal-document-types.yaml`. Select a registered type when one fits; otherwise classify a compatible occasional Product artifact as a Freeform Artifact. Apply only an assigned template.
6. Lock the reader question, scope, evidence, validity, canonical sources, structure mode, and exclusions.
7. Separate observed facts, evidence, hypotheses, accepted decisions, current company or product truth, target state, and open questions.
8. Read `../../methods/prose-quality.md`, then draft under the selected registered-type or Freeform contract. Read `../../methods/visual-production.md` when the request includes a visual or a central relationship is materially clearer as one.
9. Link Product Knowledge, Project Documentation, Technical Documentation, and Machine Authority rather than copying them.
10. Add or repair real navigation when creating or renaming a document. Do not link planned nonexistent files.
11. Verify the selected type or Freeform fallback, authority boundary, evidence, validity, links, completion contract, and absence of another public artifact's responsibility.

## Boundaries

- Own the Internal Document Types listed in `references/internal-document-types.yaml` and compatible Product Freeform Artifacts.
- Let `$Structure Product Docs` own topology, Product navigation, Responsibility READMEs, and approved migration.
- Route independently owned high-frequency artifacts without invoking their skills.
- Keep customer delivery and technical implementation in their owning authority scopes.

## Completion

The work is complete when one named company or accepted Product and one registered type or explicit Freeform fallback own the result, the applicable completion contract is satisfied, only an assigned template constrained structure, authority and validity are visible, canonical links replace duplication, real navigation exposes the artifact, and no public artifact responsibility has been absorbed.
