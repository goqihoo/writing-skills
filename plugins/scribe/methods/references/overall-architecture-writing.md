# Overall architecture writing contract

Apply this reference when `architecture-documents.md` selects `overall-architecture`. Use `../architecture-templates/assets/overall-architecture-template.md` as the output scaffold. Keep design reasoning in `../architecture-reasoning.md` and visual production in `../visual-production.md`; this reference owns the overall document's chapter boundaries, adaptation and required views.

## Establish scope and reading order

Name the whole object and its authority before choosing depth. Use the same scaffold for a simple System, a complex System, a company landscape, a Project design or a reusable reference architecture; adapt participants, evidence and commitments to that scope. Preserve a user's explicit structure. Otherwise follow the scaffold's nine-chapter reading order and approved Chinese headings, translating them for another output language.

Select depth from the reader's decisions, meaningful responsibility boundaries, interaction complexity, state and consistency, quality risks, and change needs. Keep simple designs concise and allow direct System-to-module/component explanations. Create a Subsystem document only for a coherent responsibility group with meaningful internal architectural decisions and independent reader/maintenance value. Do not derive mandatory documentation levels from deployment units, repository folders or component counts.

Use scenarios and invariants to explore normal behavior, failure and competition; derive or revise state, ownership, contracts and structure iteratively. With an existing design, start from its evidence and test it against the scenarios. Preserve the explanation order: drivers, overall solution and short working path, structure/state/contracts, then full collaboration. Model persistent state only when the design needs it; activity steps alone do not establish a state model.

Merge short adjacent sections when they answer closely related questions, preserving their obligations. Omit genuinely inapplicable content, including shared technical design or evolution when neither exists. Retain required figures for simple systems; simplicity changes their detail rather than making them optional. Apply any narrower edit request only to affected sections. Record material adaptations in the delivery note rather than cluttering the artifact with template compliance explanations.

## Keep each chapter's responsibility

1. **背景与范围** — Explain the problem and intended outcome, included/excluded responsibilities, external interactions and existing baseline. Separate observed facts, accepted decisions, proposals and assumptions. For greenfield work, describe the real starting environment and assets; for Knowledge, describe applicability rather than inventing a project history. Complete when readers can locate the boundary, understand the motivation and distinguish established starting conditions from unknowns.
2. **架构驱动** — Select functional scenarios that change architecture; identify the actor, conditions and required outcome without prescribing internal execution. Express prioritized characteristics as context, stimulus, response and observable criteria, including conflicts. Identify binding constraints and invariants with source, scope and effect; a quality target can itself be binding. Label assumed or unresolved targets instead of inventing numbers. Keep any business activity diagram at requirements level. Complete when consequential choices have identifiable drivers and satisfaction criteria.
3. **总体方案** — Explain the whole arrangement and a short representative working path before detail. State applicable principles with their local consequences. Name or precisely describe the actual style and scope, using structural evidence; diagram the system's application of that organization even when no conventional style name fits. Connect major strategies to drivers, realizing mechanisms, alternatives actually considered, accepted costs and review triggers. Distinguish inherited, accepted and proposed choices; preserve unknown historical rationale as unknown. Complete when readers can explain why the arrangement fits its drivers and what it costs.
4. **结构与职责** — Allocate responsibility, data and decision authority; distinguish storage from authority. Explain core identities, relationships, versions, authoritative/derived state, consequential transitions, guards and invariants. Allocate consistency, transaction, access, retention and reconstruction responsibilities where material. Specify significant contracts, dependency direction, local/remote and sync/async semantics, including accepted versus completed, rejection, failure and unknown outcomes. Map logical responsibilities to implementation units while leaving deployment placement to chapter 7. Complete when participants can identify their commitments and dependencies without inventing a missing owner or contract.
5. **关键流程与协作** — Name one subsection per selected key flow, not a fixed list of generic happy-path/failure chapters. Select representative flows for boundary crossings, important states, quality guarantees, irreversible effects or uncertain assumptions; use a concrete change-impact flow for a static reference model. Explain trigger, preconditions and observable result; participating actions and state handoffs; consequential alternatives and competition; and failure detection, recovery responsibility and evidence for continuation. Keep those four needs as content obligations rather than repeated mandatory subheadings. Cover partial and unknown outcomes where relevant, including guarantees weakened during recovery. Merge similar flows around a common path and material differences. Complete when each selected outcome and consequential failure can be followed through the actual design and its corresponding figure.
6. **共性技术设计** — Name subsections after actual technical concerns shared by multiple parts or flows. Explain the motivating need, scope, common rules and guarantees, mechanism, provider/consumer responsibilities and exceptions. Link the business requirement and architecture characteristic served. Keep business decisions with their owning function and state ownership in chapter 4; keep common enforcement or support mechanisms here. Select topics from the actual design rather than a universal security/transaction/logging checklist. Link existing authoritative standards and explain local application or deviation. Split a topic only for independent substance and maintenance value. Complete when each affected part can identify what it must follow, what support it receives and what it must implement itself.
7. **部署与运行** — Map logical structure to actual or proposed processes, deployment units, environments, infrastructure and failure boundaries. Explain significant startup/readiness/shutdown conditions, workload, resource limits, scaling and overload behavior; distinguish estimates from measurements. Locate detection, isolation, recovery and post-recovery correctness checks. Explain routine release order, coexistence, compatibility and stopping/recovery conditions; distinguish reversible rollback from forward repair. Reference chapter 6 mechanisms and chapter 5 local failures rather than repeating them. Keep runbook commands and complete configuration in Machine Authority. Complete when readers can relate logical design to operation, load, failure and routine change.
8. **架构验证与风险** — Trace important drivers and uncertain assumptions to methods, test conditions and pass criteria. Prioritize consequential uncertainty rather than copying all functional tests. Separate planned, partial and completed validation with evidence and limits. Record residual risks and pending decisions through trigger, affected guarantee, response and closure evidence; name owners/deadlines only when established. Complete when readers can distinguish supported claims from assumptions and judge the effect of remaining gaps.
9. **演进与迁移** — Separate committed stages from conditional directions and review triggers. Explain stage outcomes, prerequisites, viable intermediate structures and entry/exit criteria. For actual migration, locate data authority, routing, compatibility, validation, cutover, rollback limits or forward repair, and retirement. Keep project dates/tasks in their owning plan and routine releases in chapter 7. For Knowledge, explain transferable conditions and variants without invented delivery commitments. Complete when readers can follow a feasible path to the target and determine when to advance, recover or retire the old arrangement.

## Plan and verify required figures

Read `../visual-production.md` whenever this template is selected. Apply its production and render-inspection workflow internally; no separate Draw Diagram invocation is required. Let this table determine whether a figure is required, ahead of the visual method's optional complexity heuristics.

| Location | Requirement | Meaning to verify in the rendered view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.3 外部上下文 | Require an external-context figure. | Whole-object boundary, external participants/dependencies and meaningful interactions; treat internal design as a black box. |
| 3.1 方案概览 | Require an overall architecture figure. | Principal parts, responsibility boundaries and relationships at the selected scope. |
| 3.3 架构风格与组织方式 | Require a style/organization view. | Show how this object's actual layers, dependency rules, interaction mode or isolation boundaries realize the chosen organization; a generic style specimen or label is insufficient. |
| Every independently explained key flow, in any chapter | Require a corresponding sequence/flow figure for each flow. | Actors, steps, handoffs and architecturally consequential branches, competition, failure and recovery in the main or supplemental figures. Requirement scenarios in 2.1 alone do not trigger this rule. |
| 7.1 部署结构与环境 | Require a deployment figure. | Runtime units, infrastructure, communication and significant deployment/failure boundaries, with current/proposed/illustrative status clear. |
| 4.2 数据与状态 | Require a state diagram when correctness, cross-responsibility coordination or recovery depends on important state transitions defined here. | Relevant states, legal transitions and consequential guards, consistent across all affected flows; omit this requirement for stateless designs and incidental local status values. |
| 9.2 实施阶段与过渡状态 | Require transition-architecture views for complex coexistence, authority transfer or phased replacement. | Important intermediate responsibility, traffic and data ownership, not merely before/after endpoints or a calendar. |

Use data relationship, internal decomposition, dependency and other figures when existing views cannot clearly explain a material relationship. Avoid a compulsory figure for every subsection. Reuse a figure across sections only when it directly answers both reader questions; for a simple system, the overall and style explanations can reference one figure that visibly covers both composition and organizing rules. Give every independently explained key flow a corresponding identifiable diagram or panel; a general architecture image cannot stand in for it.

Embed actual diagrams near the relevant explanation and use the same participant, state and outcome names as the prose. Reference an existing rendered figure when it fully covers the required meaning; tables, textual steps, labels and empty figure slots do not satisfy a mandatory view. Keep prose responsible for conditions, guarantees and rationale while the figure exposes relations and behavior.

Honor explicit user format overrides. For a scope that genuinely has no deployment realization, such as a purely logical reference architecture, state that boundary and omit the deployment section rather than inventing infrastructure. For company scope, depict systems and operating boundaries at company scale; link system-level placement detail. Record scope-based departures in the delivery note. Missing evidence alone does not justify presenting invented topology as fact: show a supported baseline or a clearly proposed/illustrative realization and expose the unresolved choice.

## Complete the document

Review the full reading path and trace a representative scenario from driver through overall choice, state/contract ownership, collaboration and verification. Check consequential failure and recovery against the same authorities and names. Explain each shared technical rule once and retain local consequences where needed. Audit each required view and conditional trigger against the actual document, then inspect the rendered figures through the Visual Production Method. Complete only when applicable chapter questions are answered, required figures are present and coherent, evidence limits are explicit, and no unresolved design gap is concealed by a heading, placeholder or future link.
