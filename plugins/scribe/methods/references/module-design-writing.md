# Module design writing contract

Apply this reference when `architecture-documents.md` selects `module-design`. Use `../architecture-templates/assets/module-design-template.md` as the output scaffold. Keep general reasoning, evidence, prose and visual production in their existing Shared Methods; this reference owns the Module chapter boundaries, adaptation and required views.

## Establish the function and its authority

Explain how one cohesive business or technical function evaluates rules, changes state, coordinates dependencies and produces an observable result. Select independent Module Design only when the function has sufficient rules, state or coordination to warrant a coherent explanation; a code directory or helper function alone does not justify a design document. Apply the primary-document selection rules in `../architecture-documents.md`.

Let the module belong directly to a System or to a justified Subsystem. Inherit that governing authority, organizing style, principles and shared mechanisms, and explain their local consequences. Use private or shared components where meaningful without requiring component documents or another architecture level. Preserve stable versus Project purpose and reference-Knowledge applicability; do not invent deployment, historical facts or project commitments to fill the scaffold.

Follow the five-chapter reading order, with the approved second-chapter title **规则与接口**. Use **Preserve the reasoning chain** in `../architecture-documents.md` to establish the operating model in chapter 1 before expanding rules and internal mechanisms. Treat that orientation as preparation for the detailed realization in chapter 4, not a duplicate independently explained flow. Define important state before tracing its transitions and verify the resulting guarantees. Preserve explicit user structure and narrow-edit scope; merge short related explanations when their obligations remain clear. Put extension/compatibility in 3.4 and runtime effects beside affected flows rather than imposing separate platform or migration chapters.

## Keep each chapter's responsibility

1. **功能说明** — Establish a representative use, actor, conditions and expected observable result before implementation. Bound which decisions, actions, facts and results the module owns and which dependencies remain outside. Explain allocated guarantees, characteristics, constraints and the local consequences of governing principles, including decisions requiring coordination. Preserve unknown local budgets. Establish existing capabilities, behavior/data/interfaces to retain, intended changes and evidence status; combine baseline with scope when brief. Complete when readers know the function, responsibility boundary, governing requirements and design starting point.
2. **规则与接口** — Explain independently meaningful operations and when callers use them; do not force CRUD. Specify input/output identity, meaning, version and context, preconditions and observable completion, distinguishing acceptance, completed processing and external effects where relevant. Explain applicable business or technical rules: eligibility, permissions, calculations, state limits, conflicts/priorities and invalid or uncertain outcomes. Keep externally owned rules linked and explain local application; expose undecided business policy instead of inventing it. Explain local/remote and sync/async calls, dependency guarantees, retries, ordering, cancellation and compatibility where material. Retain implementation-significant semantics here while linking full field/protocol/error catalogs. Complete when callers and implementers agree on supported behavior, required rules and success/failure/unknown meanings.
3. **实现设计** — Show internal responsibility for rule evaluation, coordination and dependency access, the dependency direction and actual/proposed code organization. Keep a simple implementation when sufficient; distinguish private implementation from shared components. Establish identities, relationships, versions, authoritative/derived data, important state/guards/permissions, atomic changes, cross-boundary consistency and durable recovery/reconstruction evidence, including material access, retention and traceability requirements. Connect algorithms, storage, coordination and component choices to the allocated requirements, realizing mechanisms, applicable parent style/principles, alternatives and costs. Explain actual change needs, extension points, compatibility, transitional work/data, rollback limits and parent coordination without inventing plugin frameworks. Complete when each important rule or interface guarantee maps to an owner, data constraint and mechanism and readers can assess change impact.
4. **处理流程** — Name a subsection per selected key processing flow. Explain the triggering operation and preconditions; decision/processing order and dependency calls; state changes, atomic commits and handoffs; and failure/recovery with completed actions, result certainty, ownership and continuation evidence. Keep these four needs as content obligations, not four mandatory repeated headings. Locate important authorization checks, concurrency/repeat/order/cancellation behavior and durable control evidence at the affected steps. Explain wait, timeout, capacity, admission, backpressure/degradation and completion signals where they affect the function, with parent runtime/isolation assumptions explicit. Prefer flows exercising consequential rules, state, cross-module handoffs, competition or irreversible effects; group equivalent variants rather than diagramming every endpoint. Place errors near the steps they affect and independently useful recovery paths in their own flows. Complete when implementers can determine order, guards, commit boundaries and exception actions without inventing correctness-critical behavior.
5. **设计验证** — Verify operation results, decision/calculation/permission/state rules, conflicts/priorities and completion effects with meaningful conditions and sourced expected outcomes. Check applicable boundary, concurrency, repeat, dependency/resource, interruption/recovery and compatibility cases against the actual design; do not impose all categories on every module. Separate local verification from required real-dependency and cross-module integration evidence. Distinguish planned, partial and completed results with version/condition limits. Record unresolved rules, assumptions and choices through affected operations/guarantees, interim handling, limitations and closure evidence; route business-policy, contract or parent-guarantee changes to their actual decision owner. Keep detailed test cases in their test authority. Complete when readers can assess supported versus unverified behavior and its effect on implementation, integration and use.

## Explain internal composition and collaboration

Make 3.1 answer three questions: which cohesive parts implement this module, what each part owns, and how those parts collaborate. Identify actual or explicitly proposed internal parts before drawing. Give each part a subject-specific responsibility and the rule, result or state it owns where relevant. Use logical implementation units when code organization is undecided; distinguish these from verified packages or classes. Choose decomposition from the function's real decisions and mechanisms rather than filling a standard set of layers or processing stages.

Make the internal parts the main subject of the structure figure. Show module membership through a boundary or another unambiguous grouping; distinguish external callers, peer modules, shared mechanisms and runtime carriers. Retain external participants only where they explain an internal part's contract or dependency. Keep broader caller relationships in 2.4 and runtime placement with its owning architecture.

Show the significant relationships among internal parts, identifying the direction and meaning of calls, delegated decisions, data/result handoffs or shared-state access. Include the evidenced owner that selects or advances work when it explains their collaboration, even if that responsibility belongs to an application interface or external caller rather than a separately named component. Represent it at its actual boundary; do not invent a coordinator. A data-dependency chain alone does not explain separately controlled operations. Label relationship meanings, keep independent parts independent, and show how their results are composed. Leave branches, timing, waiting and recovery progression to chapter 4.

Organize the prose around the internal parts rather than narrating an end-to-end request or listing technologies. Introduce the decomposition and its rationale, then explain each part's specific responsibility, meaningful inputs and outputs, owned decisions or state where applicable, and the parts it uses or serves. Connect those explanations with the important handoffs: what one part supplies, how another uses it, and who coordinates their results. Use concise named paragraphs or another form suited to the actual design; let a genuinely simple implementation remain one cohesive part. Keep detailed decision branches in chapter 4 and runtime placement with its owning architecture.

Use the same part names and relationship meanings in prose, figure and processing flows. Make both prose and figure explain the composition and collaboration at their respective depth; neither substitutes for the other's missing meaning. Distinguish proposed responsibility units from verified code and explain how they could map to implementation without inventing existing packages. When internal design evidence is missing, state the unresolved division and its consequence rather than presenting an external dependency map as a completed internal design.

Complete this check when both prose and figure identify the internal parts, their distinct responsibilities and the important collaboration relationships, with rationale and realization explained in prose. Trace one supported operation through the described and pictured owners to check the structure against the flow. Revise a section that only inventories names or technologies, places all meaningful relationships outside the module, or narrates a processing sequence without establishing internal owners.

## Check operation realization and section boundaries

Apply **Review before completion** in `../architecture-documents.md` to every applicable subsection, including prose and rendered views. Use these reader judgments to distinguish content that belongs nearby but answers a different question:

- In **规则与接口**, let callers determine the supported operation, required context and next action after completion, rejection or uncertainty. Keep these semantics discoverable even when organized by operation rather than template subheading.
- In **实现选择与取舍**, explain why the selected arrangement meets an allocated requirement, what alternative matters and what cost is accepted. Move execution instructions and error recovery into the affected flow, retaining only the consequence that explains a choice here.
- In **处理流程**, trace consequential decisions and changes through the internal owners established in 3.1. Expand their rules, intermediate results, state guards and commits instead of repeatedly collapsing the module into a single “process” participant. Keep callers and peer modules at the boundary; reuse an external collaboration view only where it answers the local question. Preserve a genuinely simple operation without manufacturing extra participants. Group equivalent paths and reference shared steps rather than retelling the whole system journey.
- In **数据与状态**, distinguish an operation's state from the aggregate request when independent work can coexist. Define how running, waiting, partial results and completion combine; align entry selection, state views and flows with those meanings. Treat a diagram's transition guard as part of the contract, not a condition to be repaired by unrelated prose.
- In **设计验证**, keep planned checks with methods and expected observations, and actual evidence with results. Make remaining gaps specific to this module's operation and guarantee using the shared review's gap disposition; replace generic supplier/runtime reminders when they do not identify this module's uncertainty.

Test materially different supported entries against the same rules, data, flows, figures and verification obligations. Follow each through its actual owner, required identity and continuation; check that a combined path's condition has not become mandatory elsewhere for an independent operation. After repairing an ownership or precondition mismatch, recheck its remaining uses and linked owning contracts, not just the paragraph that exposed it. Apply the shared gap disposition when the sources leave the relationship unresolved; wording alone cannot settle a design decision. Complete when each subsection advances its reader judgment and each tested path has consistent support or an explicitly bounded gap.

## Required views

Apply `../visual-production.md` internally when selecting this template; apply its production/render-inspection workflow and the mandatory-view review in `../architecture-documents.md`. Let these requirements decide what must be drawn before applying optional complexity heuristics.

| Location | Requirement | Meaning to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 内部结构与职责 | Require an internal composition and collaboration figure. | Apply **Explain internal composition and collaboration** above; select the visual type through the shared Visual Production Method. |
| Every independently explained key flow, in any chapter | Require a corresponding flow or sequence figure. | Decisions/actions and guards for processing, or participants and handoffs for collaboration, including consequential branches and failure/recovery in the main or supplemental figures. |
| 3.2 数据与状态 | Require a state diagram when important transitions affect correctness, cross-module collaboration or recovery. | Relevant states, legal transitions and conditions consistent with rules and flows; omit this requirement for stateless functions or incidental local values. |

Keep chapter 1 and chapter 5 figures optional. Use a caller/dependency figure in 2.4 when the responsibility relationships are difficult to explain clearly; do not impose the Overall/Subsystem external-context or deployment requirements on Module Design. Add data relationship or other views when the actual model needs them. Give independently explained migration/cutover/recovery flows a corresponding figure even when they appear under extension and compatibility. Explain complex migration through actual transitional ownership and unfinished work; use the governing architecture or an independently useful supporting design where it owns the broader change.

Keep names, state meanings and outcomes consistent with chapters 2 and 3. Supplement overly generic steps such as “validate, process, save” with the decision conditions, transaction boundaries and failure meanings that determine correctness. Reference or reuse an existing actual figure only when it answers the local question; textual steps, placeholders and a general structure picture do not satisfy a key-flow view. Explain scope-based departures in the delivery note and preserve explicit output-format choices.

## Complete the document

Trace a consequential operation from governing requirement through its rule/interface, internal owner and state/data constraints, key flow and validation. Apply the internal composition and collaboration check to the rendered 3.1 figure. Check competition or failure against the same completion semantics and dependency assumptions. Locate the source or proposal status of each material rule and confirm no missing business policy, numeric criterion or successful verification was invented. Inspect the required rendered views and all applicable chapter completion conditions. Finish when the function is implementable at the requested depth, evidence limits are visible, and detailed code/schema/configuration/test authority remains linked rather than duplicated.
