# Prose quality method

Apply this method proportionally to every human-readable passage produced by a Public Skill. Keep the owning skill's genre, structure, facts, reasoning, precision, decisions, and completion contract intact.

## Reader-flow pass

1. Name the primary reader, what they already know, the question, and the understanding, judgment, or action reading should enable. Calibrate explanation to that reader rather than assuming either expert knowledge or no prior knowledge.
2. Establish the artifact plan before prose work. Preserve the owning skill's artifact type, required outline, section responsibilities and authorized template branches; order explanations within them by what the reader needs to understand next.
3. Place facts, reasoning, and examples in the sections that own them. Establish the subject and relationships needed to interpret a claim before relying on it. Treat templates as coverage contracts rather than a reason to defer essential orientation to a later chapter.
4. Separate facts, interpretations, choices, assumptions, and open questions. Connect consequential claims to evidence, reasoning, or an example.
5. Place prerequisites and qualifications where the reader needs them to interpret the action or claim. Establish the applicable behavior before expanding its exceptions; lead with a limitation when it changes whether that behavior applies. Keep exclusions proportional to the misunderstanding they prevent.
6. Revise reader-facing prose with the contract below after artifact logic and substance are stable.
7. Recheck every name, date, number, term, citation, qualification, requirement, acceptance criterion, decision, constraint, premise, and conclusion. Preserve precision when expression and precision conflict.
8. Remove wording or formatting that does not change understanding, use, or judgment without removing a required premise, reasoning step, boundary, or conclusion.

## Prose contract

- **Point first.** Open with the fact, action, judgment, or consequence the reader needs. Use document narration only when it changes how the reader should use the material.
- **Concrete before abstract.** Give each unfamiliar abstraction a recognizable object, event, decision, or result. Explain what it refers to in this passage, not merely what other abstract category it belongs to. Where the meaning depends on a process, state who does what, to what, under which relevant conditions, and with what result; include only the elements needed to understand the claim. Preserve useful conceptual language once its meaning is established.
- **Reader-calibrated terms.** Use familiar, precise words when they carry the same meaning. Retain necessary technical or formal terms and briefly explain them at first use when the reader is unlikely to know them. Use one stable name for one concept; distinguish related concepts explicitly instead of alternating near-synonyms. Treat jargon as a reader-context problem, not a universal banned-word list.
- **Readable references.** Prefer descriptive names over invented codes or single-letter aliases in explanatory prose. Introduce a short identifier only when it materially helps repeated reference or distinction; define it at first use, pair it with its name when needed, and keep one meaning across the document set, examples and figures. Preserve established API names, standard abbreviations, mathematical notation and externally governed identifiers. Do not imply that an illustrative label is an implemented field or interface.
- **Meaningful qualifiers.** Make consequential labels such as valid, appropriate, sufficient, or advanced understandable from the surrounding text. State the relevant criterion when it determines inclusion, comparison, action, or outcome. If the source does not supply that criterion, identify the uncertainty or seek clarification when necessary; do not invent a threshold or silently remove the qualification.
- **Causal movement.** Move adjacent sentences by cause, consequence, contrast, condition, or sequence. Name that relationship instead of merely announcing the next topic.
- **Human syntax.** Name actors and use ordinary, exact verbs. Unpack noun chains and sentences carrying several independent relationships.
- **Natural expression.** Write as a competent human addressing the intended audience in the actual relationship and setting. Match the register to how the passage will be read or spoken. Express shared meaning as a natural, direct sentence. Enumerate only when the individual items are necessary to the reader's task. Remove prompt-like framing, mechanical parallelism, slogan fragments, and language that exposes the template or production process.
- **Selective structure.** Preserve explicitly required headings and fields, but expose only useful scaffolding. Name other sections for the actual subject and the relationship, behavior or decision they explain. Make a title specific enough, in its parent context, to predict the section’s contribution; broad labels such as implementation, processing or mechanism need a concrete referent. Merge a section whose answer is already complete nearby. Keep necessary technical names when they identify the actual subject rather than replacing them with vague synonyms. Use prose to develop explanations and causal arguments, diagrams to expose relationships or behavior, and lists for genuinely parallel items or ordered steps. Use tables for compact comparison, lookup, matrices or traceable records whose repeated fields help the reader. Explain mechanisms and consequential judgments outside the cells; expand sentence-heavy cells into the owning explanation instead of converting them into a long bullet list. Choose formats by reader task, without a fixed table quota.
- **Proportionate emphasis.** Give more space to consequential or difficult ideas without manufacturing mirrored sections, uniform paragraphs, or formulaic lists.
- **Grounded examples.** Use examples to clarify the claim owned by a section. Carry one example across sections only when the artifact plan gives it that job.
- **Honest qualification.** Keep material uncertainty, exceptions, assumptions, and limits close to the claim they constrain.
- **Consistent voice.** Treat a supplied writing sample as the strongest guide. Otherwise preserve the writer's relationship to the reader and level of formality without inventing anecdotes, emotions, opinions, quotations, or deliberate imperfections.
- **Clean delivery.** Cut canned openings, generic importance claims, vague authority, promotional gloss, exposed template instructions, redundant summaries, and conversational handoffs the genre does not need.

Scan for reader-flow failures before delivery: an opening about the document instead of the subject; several unexplained abstract nouns arriving together; labels whose criteria matter but remain unstated; internal workflow language standing in for an explanation; consecutive paragraphs with the same sentence pattern; repeated template labels used as prose; and transitions that announce sections instead of relationships. Rewrite each failure inside the locked artifact plan. Keep the explanation needed for the reader's current question nearby; place implementation detail in its owning section or reference when it would interrupt that explanation. Preserve required structure and do not impose a single outline on different genres.

## Keep working checks separate from reader-facing content

Keep checks about following a skill, avoiding scope expansion or choosing a template in working notes. During the final edit, remove sentences whose only contribution is explaining why a path, reference, threshold or approval was not invented; put a relevant workflow limitation in the delivery note. Preserve the actual missing dependency, undecided budget or unverified guarantee beside the design it affects. Present the conclusions readers need: establish the document-wide scope, baseline and status once, then place consequential local assumptions, uncertainty and limitations beside the affected claim. Preserve security, authorization, audit and recovery controls as substantive design content; an internal writing check and a system control have different readers and purposes. Retain a repeated qualification only where omitting it would make the local passage misleading.

For Chinese technical explanations, read `references/prose-examples-zh.md` during expression review. Use its source-bounded examples to clarify actors, actions and conditions while preserving terminology and evidence. Treat missing mechanism details as gaps or labeled proposals, not facts supplied by a style rewrite.

## Whole-document expression review

Read the completed document in order, including the transition into and out of each figure, table and list. At each major transition, paraphrase what the intended reader can now understand using only their assumed knowledge and the explanation reached so far. Locate the first point that requires a later definition, an unexplained actor or relationship, or a return to scattered passages to assemble the basic account. Distinguish a useful reference for deeper detail from a missing premise for the current explanation. Repair the introduction, local order or connection at that point and reread the affected path; do not fix it by adding the same summary to every section. Preserve explicit structure while adjusting explanation within it.

Edit the complete argument before polishing isolated sentences. For each section and paragraph, identify the understanding or decision it adds to what the reader already knows. Consolidate repeated claims into their primary explanation; keep a recurrence when it applies the established idea to a new mechanism, consequence or decision. Repair a missing connection before polishing the adjacent sentences. Make actors and applicable conditions recoverable locally; resolve generic references such as “the module” or “the caller” when they could refer to different owners. Preserve technical terms and split independently important claims without fragmenting the causal account.

After deleting or merging content, reread the actual sources beside each complete standalone artifact, not just its outline, working notes or companion document. Account for each source requirement that constrains the requested design by locating its explanation in that artifact or recording why it is outside scope in working notes. Preserve source-mandated information needed to implement a mechanism, including persistent recovery state, without copying full executable schemas. Then compare against the section plan. Restore any lost responsibility, condition, state transition, transaction or concurrency guarantee, permission, failure behavior, uncertainty or evidence distinction. Keep a useful reminder when a reader needs it at a separate decision point; brevity alone is not the acceptance criterion. Complete this edit when each consequential passage adds a clear answer and no material constraint was lost or invented.

For each table, identify its distinct reader task and compare its information with neighboring prose, figures and other tables. Keep the table when simultaneous comparison or lookup materially helps and cells stay compact. Consolidate repeated facts into their owning explanation; retain a brief summary only when it serves a distinct navigation or decision task. Explain process, causality and recovery in connected prose or an appropriate diagram, then use a compact index only if readers still need one. Avoid restating every row as a paragraph or replacing a table with an equally fragmented list.

Review the aggregate effect as well as each individual table. Several individually defensible tables can still fragment the argument or repeat the same responsibilities under different labels. Reorganize around the reader's questions until the main reasoning is continuous and each retained structured block contributes a distinct function. Preserve requirements, qualifications, decisions, identifiers, evidence states and source relationships when merging content. Evaluate substantial revisions on an actual full-length document at the intended scope; short excerpts can check local expression but do not establish whole-document readability.

Keep code, commands, formulas, schemas, exact diagram labels, diagram syntax, structured data, quotations, and exact-format text outside this method.

## Completion checks

- One primary reader task resolves through a clear reading path; finding its answer somewhere in the complete document does not alone establish that path.
- Facts, interpretations, choices, assumptions, and open questions remain distinct.
- Important conclusions state their evidence and boundary concretely.
- No fact, agreement, or material decision is invented or hidden.
- The heading sequence, section responsibilities, and reasoning path match the artifact plan.
- The point arrives early, abstractions have concrete anchors, sentences move through explicit relationships, and formatting serves the content.
- The intended reader can paraphrase each consequential passage: what it refers to, what it says about that subject, and which conditions affect its meaning. For instructions, they can also identify the required action and expected result. Repair unclear meaning rather than merely shortening sentences; preserve genre, voice, precision, and uncertainty.
- Readers can tell when to act and when the result needs revision.
