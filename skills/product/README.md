# Product

Skills for product lifecycle decisions, knowledge-set structure, and product artifacts.

## Lifecycle and structure

- [assess-product-lifecycle](assess-product-lifecycle/SKILL.md) — Assess the next decision, evidence sufficiency, artifact coverage, and readiness.
- [structure-product-docs](structure-product-docs/SKILL.md) — Plan, scaffold, audit, or migrate a product knowledge set around the seven-directory core.

## Common product documents

- [write-product-doc](write-product-doc/SKILL.md) — Select and create one common product map, reference, behavior, evidence, decision, or release-notes document.

## Standard product artifacts

- [write-product-strategy](write-product-strategy/SKILL.md) — Define product direction, users, value, choices, non-goals, and review triggers.
- [map-product-capabilities](map-product-capabilities/SKILL.md) — Model durable product capabilities, relationships, authority, and composition.
- [write-solution-definition](write-solution-definition/SKILL.md) — Define a reusable capability composition for a class of product scenarios.
- [write-product-roadmap](write-product-roadmap/SKILL.md) — Sequence outcomes, product bets, capability gaps, dependencies, and decisions.
- [write-prd](write-prd/SKILL.md) — Align one product change's problem, scope, behavior, and acceptance.
- [design-product-metrics](design-product-metrics/SKILL.md) — Define product outcomes, metric semantics, guardrails, and governance.
- [write-release-plan](write-release-plan/SKILL.md) — Plan product release readiness, coordination, success, and stop conditions.
- [write-product-review](write-product-review/SKILL.md) — Evaluate product evidence and record the next accepted decisions.
