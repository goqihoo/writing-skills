# Architecture decision questions

Ask only when an answer can materially change scope, ownership, contracts, controls, topology, cost, risk, rollout, or validation. Proceed with a labeled reversible assumption for smaller gaps.

Use this form:

### Decision: <short name>

- **Why now:** What cannot be finalized safely?
- **Known context:** Confirmed facts and constraints.
- **Options:** Two or three viable choices with comparable tradeoffs.
- **Recommendation:** Preferred option and evidence, when available.
- **Impact:** Boundaries, contracts, controls, cost, risk, or validation affected.
- **Needed from:** The stakeholder with authority or missing facts.
- **Safe interim assumption:** Include only when reversible.

Track the question as `open`, `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deferred`, or `superseded`. Once resolved, replace the question in the main narrative with the accepted decision and preserve its cost and review trigger.
