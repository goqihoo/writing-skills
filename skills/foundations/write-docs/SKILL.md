# Write Docs

Make the document help one primary reader understand, decide, act, verify, or find an answer.

## Workflow

1. **Set the outcome.** Name the primary reader, the question, and the action reading should enable.
2. **Respect the artifact.** Follow its owning skill and healthy local conventions. Preserve verified facts, links, decisions, and scope.
3. **Build the reader path.** Order sections by the questions readers must answer, not by the order of research.
4. **Support each claim.** Separate facts, interpretations, choices, assumptions, and open questions. Connect important claims to evidence, reasoning, or an example.
5. **State boundaries.** Expose prerequisites, non-goals, exceptions, failure modes, uncertainty, and conditions that would change the conclusion.
6. **Write concretely.** Name actors, actions, conditions, and observable results. Explain each new key term with its purpose and one example.
7. **Compress and verify.** Remove material that does not change use or judgment. Test the result against the reader action and the owning skill's completion criteria.

## Shared completion check

- One primary reader task is resolved through a clear path.
- Facts, interpretations, choices, assumptions, and open questions remain distinct.
- Important conclusions state their evidence and boundary in concrete language.
- No fact, agreement, or material decision is invented or hidden.
- Readers can tell when to act and when the document needs revision.
