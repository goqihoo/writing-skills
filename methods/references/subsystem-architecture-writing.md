# Subsystem architecture writing contract

Apply this reference when `architecture-documents.md` selects `subsystem-architecture`. Use `../architecture-templates/assets/subsystem-architecture-template.md` as the output scaffold. Keep general reasoning, evidence, prose and visual production under their existing Shared Methods; this reference owns the Subsystem chapter boundaries, adaptation and required views.

## Locate the responsibility

Explain how this responsibility group realizes commitments allocated by its parent. Justify independent Subsystem architecture through a coherent responsibility boundary, meaningful internal architectural decisions and independent reader/maintenance value. Apply the primary-document selection rules in `../architecture-documents.md`; a repository folder, deployment unit or module count does not establish a documentation level.

Inherit the parent's established constraints, principles, shared mechanisms and authority through references, then explain their local consequences. Distinguish local refinements from changes requiring parent coordination. Preserve actual System/Subsystem versus Project authority; use explicit applicability assumptions for reference Knowledge without inventing a deployed parent or project. Follow the seven-chapter scaffold unless an explicit user structure or concrete scope adaptation applies. Merge concise adjacent explanations where useful, retaining their content obligations. Omit chapter 7 when there is no substantive evolution or migration to explain; do not manufacture construction stages for a new Subsystem.

## Keep each chapter's responsibility

1. **定位与设计要求** — Establish the Subsystem's contribution, included/excluded responsibility, governing architecture and reason for independent design. Explain allocated scenarios and characteristics, sourced local targets, inherited constraints and invariants, decision boundaries, and the observed starting point. Keep unallocated budgets visible rather than assigning the whole parent's budget to this part. Separate current facts, accepted decisions, proposals and assumptions; leave solution choices to chapter 3. Complete when readers know which outcomes and guarantees this part owns and which decisions it may change locally.
2. **外部协作** — Treat the Subsystem as a black box. Identify callers, event/data sources and dependencies, whether in-process or remote. Explain provided capabilities through the point of responsibility transfer and the duties retained by callers or dependencies. Specify architecture-significant input/precondition, identity/version, sync/async, completion/rejection/failure/unknown, retry/order/cancellation and compatibility semantics where relevant; link full interface specifications. Complete when callers can use the Subsystem correctly, infer what a result permits, and identify their responsibilities after an exception.
3. **内部方案** — Show the internal structure and a short working path before detailed state. Explain the actual organizing style and dependency rules, including inheritance or local refinement of the parent style; no distinct local style is required. Allocate responsibilities and internal contracts that jointly realize the external promise. Establish core identities, relations, versions, authoritative/derived data, state meanings, legal transitions, guards, atomicity and cross-boundary consistency; preserve material recovery, access, retention, reconstruction and audit requirements. Connect principles, strategies and technology choices to allocated characteristics, mechanisms, costs, alternatives and review triggers. Keep rationale beside a local mechanism and consolidate broader tradeoffs in 3.4. Complete when each important external promise has identifiable internal owners, contracts and realizing mechanisms.
4. **关键流程与协作** — Name a subsection per actual key flow. Trace trigger, preconditions and observable completion through internal participants, state changes, transactions and handoffs; explain consequential alternatives/competition and failure/recovery with ownership and continuation evidence. Keep those four content needs within the flow rather than imposing four repeated subheadings. Select flows that exercise external promises, important state or transaction boundaries, quality risks or uncertain assumptions. Use a change-impact flow for static reference models. Reference chapter 3 state definitions and mechanisms, and add internal detail to a parent's scenario rather than copying it. Treat an independently useful recovery path as its own flow; merge equivalent variants around a common path. Complete when readers can follow completion and a consequential failure without inventing a missing action or guarantee.
5. **运行保障** — Locate this Subsystem within the parent's real or proposed runtime; do not assume separate deployment. Explain shared/exclusive resources, startup/readiness/shutdown, capacity and concurrency assumptions. State dependency/overload effects on externally visible guarantees, isolation, rejection or degradation, health versus mere process liveness, operating signals and recovery correctness. Reference governing logging, tracing, backup and security mechanisms while retaining local integration duties. Locate runtime identities and access boundaries here, internal control responsibility in chapter 3, and consequential checks in chapter 4. Explain routine release, mixed-version compatibility, change order, verification and rollback limits or forward repair; leave structural migration to chapter 7 and commands/configuration to Machine Authority. Complete when readers can determine placement, dependencies, limits and guarantees under failure and routine change.
6. **架构验证与风险** — Verify allocated characteristics and external contracts, the composition of internal state/transaction/recovery mechanisms, and actual runtime/dependency conditions. Record object, method, conditions and pass criteria for consequential checks. Separate planned, partial and completed evidence and its limits. Distinguish proof of the allocated local guarantee from parent end-to-end composition; identify who supplies evidence for caller/dependency assumptions. Record residual risks through triggers, affected guarantees, response and closure evidence, distinguishing local resolution, parent coordination and external dependencies. Use known owners/timing only. Complete when readers can assess which commitments are supported and whether remaining gaps affect the parent.
7. **演进与迁移** — When applicable, separate committed work from conditional review triggers. Explain local versus externally visible changes, viable coexistence, routing, data authority, compatibility and stage conditions. Preserve unfinished requests, jobs or sessions through completion, transfer or controlled termination; static data migration alone is insufficient. Explain migration validation, cutover, rollback limits or forward repair, and retirement conditions. Reference parent staging and dependencies while owning local transition mechanisms; distinguish these from routine releases in chapter 5. Complete when each transition has an accountable owner, preserved or explicitly weakened commitments, an advance condition and a recovery path protecting established results.

## Required views

Apply `../visual-production.md` internally whenever this template is selected. Let the following requirements determine which views to produce; optional complexity heuristics do not remove mandatory views. Keep figure rendering/inspection and format selection in that method, and apply the mandatory-view review in `../architecture-documents.md`.

| Location | Requirement | Meaning to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 调用方与依赖 | Require a Subsystem context figure. | Black-box boundary, callers/dependencies and meaningful interactions, including in-process peers where relevant. |
| 3.1 内部结构与工作方式 | Require an internal architecture figure. | Actual parts, relationships and organizing rules; show layers, dependency direction or interaction mode rather than just a module inventory. |
| Every independently explained key flow, in any chapter | Require a corresponding sequence/flow figure. | Participants, actions, handoffs and consequential branches, concurrency, failure/recovery, in the main or supplemental figures. |
| 5.1 运行位置与资源 | Require a runtime/deployment view within the parent. | Placement, runtime units, dependencies and significant shared resources; depict an in-process Subsystem as in-process. |
| 3.3 数据与状态 | Require a state diagram when important transitions affect correctness, cross-responsibility collaboration or recovery. | States, legal transitions and guards consistent with the flows; incidental local status values or stateless behavior do not trigger this requirement. |
| 7.2 过渡方案与兼容 | Require transition-architecture views for complex coexistence, authority transfer or phased replacement. | Intermediate responsibilities, traffic, data authority and ownership of unfinished work where relevant. |

Use additional data, dependency, mechanism or validation figures only when they materially clarify an otherwise unclear relationship. A first-chapter figure is not mandatory; establish the context in 2.1. Reuse or reference a real parent/runtime or internal/style figure when it fully answers the local reader question. Keep the Subsystem boundary and local consequences identifiable rather than assuming a parent overview is sufficient. Give each independently explained key flow an identifiable corresponding diagram or panel; an architecture picture or textual steps do not satisfy its flow requirement.

Keep participants, state and completion names consistent across prose and figures. A pure logical reference without a runtime realization may omit deployment with an explicit scope explanation in the delivery note; a concrete in-process Subsystem still needs its placement explained. Preserve explicit user structure/format and narrow-edit scope. State material omissions or equivalent figure reuse in the delivery note, and remove only genuinely unused scaffold slots.

## Complete the document

Trace one consequential external promise and failure from its source through internal owners, contracts, data/state, key flow, runtime assumptions and validation. Check the effect on the parent's guarantee and preserve unknown allocations or dependency behavior as gaps. Verify all applicable chapter completion conditions and inspect the actual required figures. Finish when the local realization is coherent, authority and evidence remain explicit, and the reader can understand the internal design without rereading a duplicate of the parent architecture.
